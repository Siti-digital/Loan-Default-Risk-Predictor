Methodology & Statistical Defenses

Zero Look-Ahead Bias

Macro indicators are lagged by 1 month and merged using pd.merge_asof on origination date, guaranteeing no future economic information leaks into historical loan decisions.

Chronological Out-of-Time (OOT) Split

Models are trained on 2013–2016 originations and tested on 2017 originations. Standard K-fold cross-validation is avoided to prevent temporal correlation leakage.

Econometric Odds Ratios

Coefficients from statsmodels.api.Logit allow direct derivation of odds multipliers (eβ), proving macroeconomic significance with rigorous p-values.

Probability Calibration

Class imbalance is handled via balanced loss weighting and validated using PR-AUC and Brier Score Loss to confirm reliable probability outputs.
